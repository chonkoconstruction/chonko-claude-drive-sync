**"No data ≠ no demand."** Many real services sit below SE Ranking's reporting floor (e.g. `outdoor kitchen columbia sc`, which Chonko *already ranks for*). Sub-threshold terms are captured as **long-tail sections \+ GBP \+ town-named copy**, never as volume-justified standalone pages.

# 1\. Competitive Positioning Statement

Chonko's durable moat is **cross-vertical, full-service general contracting under one brand** — decks, fences, patios, kitchen/bath/additions, site work, concrete, and now custom homes — paired with the deepest, freshest content in the market (170+ blog posts, active to 2026\) and a **pricing-transparency wedge no competitor in any vertical occupies**. The corpus confirms that across all seven verticals, **not one Columbia competitor publishes real cost ranges** — the single biggest content opportunity on the site.

The competitive field, vertical by vertical:

- **Decks / outdoor living — Archadeck of Central SC is the one real wall.** A 25-year franchise with 13 service pages, TimberTech Platinum \+ AZEK, published price floors, and the "Columbia's Best Deck Builder" footprint — it appears in \~9 of the outdoor SERPs. Everyone else (Pioneer Deck, Deck Pro, Blight Built, Green Exterior) is GBP-thin with negligible content. **Carolina Gold is actively exiting residential** (its sitemap is now 5 commercial URLs; its \#2 deck and \#2 screened-porch pages are decaying) — opening a direct capture window.  
- **Remodeling — a moat, because every competitor is partial-scope.** Capital Kitchen & Bath (\#1 kitchen) is a design showroom; Palmetto Bath and Re-Bath (\#1 bath) are product-replacement franchises winning on national domain authority, not custom remodeling; Bald Cypress and PCR are the closest design-build parallels but **none of them touches outdoor living, site work, concrete, or custom homes.** Chonko is the only firm that can serve "remodel my kitchen *and* build my deck *and* clear my lot."  
- **Fences — thin and uncontested on content/cost.** Seegars wins on 75-year credentials (AFA Contractor of the Year), Superior F\&R on 32k national reviews, Boundless Barriers on taxonomy breadth — but the top *organic* position (Ryan Brewer, \#2–3) is a homepage-only site, and **nobody has a cost guide or per-material cost content.** \~900–1,000/mo of geo demand sits behind weak content.  
- **Concrete — thin local field, the highest-value click in the dataset.** Duffie and Venture Concrete win on homepage authority; neither has a dedicated, content-rich `concrete driveway` page despite the **$18.06 CPC** on that term.  
- **Site services — nearly uncontested.** Chonko already ranks **\#2 for grading/excavation** and **\#1 (SGE) for "clearing and grubbing."** Every "competitor" ranking for land clearing/grading is either a tree service (different trade) or a GBP-only operator with \~1 organic visit/month.  
- **Custom homes — one beatable in-market player.** Build On Your Land LLC drives traffic mostly from national/branded queries; its Columbia page is generic and its trust stack is empty (no BBB, license, warranty, or certifications). Chonko's full trust stack \+ true custom design-build (vs. BOYL's 28-floor-plan select model) \+ real Midlands photography wins.

**Where Chonko does not chase volume:** new-construction/spec **listings** terms (`new homes columbia sc` 590/mo, KD 34–37 — Zillow/NewHomeSource/portal-owned), `deck builder lake murray sc` (dock-builder SERP), electric/dog-fence (off-trade), commercial GC, and ready-mix/material-supplier terms.

# 4\. Complete URL Architecture — keyword-based slugs & hubs *(the core of v1.1)*

### Slug rules (v1.1 — supersedes v1.0's conservative geo rule)

- **All lowercase, hyphen-separated, ASCII, no underscores, no spaces.** Max \~5 words / \~60 chars where possible.  
- **Hub landings: clean topical slugs, no geo** — `/decks-patios-fences/`, `/remodeling-renovations/`, `/site-services/`, `/custom-homes/`, `/service-areas/`.  
- **Commercial sub-service "money" pages: geo-qualified slug `service-columbia-sc`.** Rationale, all three from the corpus: (1) the measured commercial keywords *are* geo-qualified ("X columbia sc"); (2) Chonko's one already-ranking slug is geo'd (`deck-builder-columbia-sc`); (3) the audit explicitly recommends adding geo to the non-geo slugs (`fence-installation`, `concrete-paver-contractor`, most site-services slugs). This **supersedes** v1.0's "geo only where strictly part of the keyword."  
- **Use the searched plural/natural form** in the slug where it is the measured term: `fence-companies-columbia-sc` (not "fence-builders"), `concrete-contractors-columbia-sc`, `deck-builders-columbia-sc` for new pages.  
- **Material/variant pages (e.g., fence materials) nest under the geo'd money page** with short clean slugs (`/wood-fences/`) — they target body-copy modifiers and national cost questions, not stacked geo.  
- **Drop-"sc" variants are an on-page/H2 device, not a slug device** (e.g. `columbia home remodeling` KD 5, `concrete columbia` KD 3 are lower-KD but reads worse as a URL). Use them in copy/headings, not slugs.  
- **`/site-services/` — never `/siteservices/`** (the current live error).

### Trailing-slash decision (kept)

The live, indexed URLs all use **trailing slashes** (confirmed in `chonko-site-audit.md`). Switching conventions would force a sitewide redirect for zero SEO gain. **Decision: keep trailing slashes; set WordPress permalinks to trailing-slash and 301 the non-trailing form to it.** (This is a live-site/technical decision, independent of any other document.)

# STANDARDS BLOCK — On-Page, Architecture & Methodology

*These are the standards for the **next** phase (titles → meta → schema → content). They are referenced by Doc 3 and built from this document. Title/meta writing is the step **after** this URL architecture is locked.*

## S1 — Title Tag Formula (50–60 chars) *(next-phase)*

`[Primary Keyword] | [Differentiator or Town] | Chonko Construction` Use the **measured searched phrasing** front-loaded; brand last; never exceed 60 chars. Examples (illustrative, written next phase):

- Fence: `Fence Companies in Columbia SC | Chonko Construction`  
- Bathroom: `Bathroom Remodel in Columbia SC | Custom, Not Liners | Chonko`  
- Concrete driveway: `Concrete Driveways in Columbia SC | Chonko Construction`  
- Custom home: `Custom Home Builder in Columbia SC | Chonko Construction`

## S2 — Meta Description Formula (140–160 chars) *(next-phase)*

`[Primary keyword] + [town/area] + [differentiator/price/trust hook] + [CTA verb]` (Call / Book / Get / See). One per page; no duplication.

## S3 — Slug Construction *(updated v1.1)*

Per §4 rules: clean hub slugs; **geo-qualified `service-columbia-sc` slugs on commercial money pages**; searched plural/natural form; material/variant pages nested with short clean slugs; drop-"sc" reserved for copy/H2, not slugs.

## S4 — Image Filename \+ Alt Text *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 §3.9")*

- **Filename:** `service-location-descriptor.webp` → `composite-deck-lexington-sc-backyard.webp`. Lowercase, hyphenated, WebP.  
- **Alt text:** natural descriptive sentence with service \+ location where it reads naturally — `Composite deck Chonko built in a Lexington SC backyard`. Every image; no stuffing. (Reinforces the real-photography EEAT moat.)

## S5 — Internal Linking \+ Anchor Distribution *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 §4.6")*

- Minimum **2 internal links per page.**  
- **60 / 30 / 10 anchor mix:** 60% exact/partial-match keyword of the destination; 30% related/partial variations; 10% branded/natural. Keeps the anchor profile natural.  
- Blog → money-page links follow Doc 3's assigned anchors.  
- **Cluster wiring to enforce (from the corpus):** stump-grinding → fence \+ deck \+ land-clearing (site-prep funnel); land-clearing ↔ grading ↔ "clearing & grubbing" \#1 blog; paver-patio ↔ concrete-driveway via the "pavers vs. concrete" comparison; deck money page → deck-repair; fence money page → material pages; screened-porch ↔ deck \+ patio \+ (sunroom comparison).

## S6 — Title/Meta CTR-Rewrite Protocol *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 Part 8")*

Trigger a **title \+ meta rewrite first** (before any body change) when **either**: (a) 200+ impressions/30 days with **CTR \< 1.5%**, or (b) **position 5–15 with \~0 clicks**. Re-measure 30 days, then touch body. **Day-one targets:** the kitchen \+ bathroom pages (pos 19, the Page-2 Goldmine) and the page-1/0-click `site-preparation` \+ `grading-excavation` pages.

## S7 — Three-Tier Content Architecture *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 §4.11")*

- **L1 — Money/Service Pages (Pillars):** the §4 sub-service pages. Final conversion destination.  
- **L2 — Blog Category Hubs:** mini-pillars (300–500-word intro, link to 8–12 top L3 posts, one link up to the L1 with the L1's measured primary keyword as anchor).  
- **L3 — Individual Posts:** link up to exactly one L2 and one L1; optional lateral link to 1–2 related L3. No footer keyword-link blocks.  
- **Capacity:** the current L2 taxonomy supports \~600–800 unique posts before any sub-topic's keyword space is exhausted.

## S8 — Five Quality Gates *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 §4.12")*

Every blog post passes: **(1) Local Specificity** (fails if city names removed); **(2) Originality** (information gain; keyword not already claimed in the register); **(3) Factual Accuracy** (every stat/price/spec verified against a primary source — `[HUMAN]` checkpoint); **(4) Voice & EEAT** (named-author review); **(5) Structural Compliance** (Quick-Answer block, internal links, schema, word count, title/meta lengths).

## S9 — Keyword Methodology (for the Scout Agent, Doc 5\) *(Doc 3 cites as "Doc 2 Part 1"; this \+ §3 \= the keyword map)*

For any page/post the Scout Agent returns: **primary keyword (exact, measured)** · 2–3 secondary · intent (L/C/I/N) · **measured volume \+ KD \+ CPC** · L1+L2 assignment. Before assignment, check the master register and the near-duplicate rule (two keywords are duplicates if they share all content words, produce the same H1/FAQ/pricing table, map to the same L1+town, or return the same top-5 SERP). **When ambiguous, block** — a page not written costs nothing; cannibalization costs rankings. **Measured-data discipline:** never prioritize a term on volume that returned "no data" (use it as long-tail capture); always use the *searched* phrasing (Appendix A).

# APPENDIX A — Phrasing Rules (load-bearing: use the searched form)

The measured data shows plural/natural forms register where singular/agency forms do not. Use the **left column** in slugs, H1s, and titles.

| :---- | :---- |
| fence **companies** columbia sc · fencing columbia sc | fence builders columbia sc |
| concrete **contractors / companies** columbia sc | (singular often weaker) |
| deck **builders** columbia sc | deck contractor columbia sc |
| **sunrooms** columbia sc | sunroom contractor / builder columbia sc |
| deck **repair** columbia sc | deck replacement columbia sc |
| **screened in porch** columbia sc | screened porch builder columbia sc |
